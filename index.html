日常好生活小店

HOT XU explores a fresh approach to everyday retail, bringing food and daily essentials into a friendly, thoughtfully designed store.

圆润的 H 图形与薄荷绿、青柠黄形成鲜明识别，结合自然材质与柔和光线，延展至门店、包装和日常物料。
